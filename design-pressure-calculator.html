Design Pressure Estimator

Windows & doors — ASCE 7 components & cladding method · Majestic Permits
Estimation tool only — not for permit submission. This uses simplified, approximate coefficient values to give a ballpark design pressure. Actual permit-ready calculations must be performed with the official ASCE 7 tables/figures (or engineering software) and sealed by a Florida-licensed engineer. For product selection, match against the window/door's tested DP rating on its Miami-Dade NOA, Broward BC approval, or Florida Product Approval — don't install based on this number alone.
