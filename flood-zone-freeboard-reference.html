Flood Zone & Freeboard Reference

General education on FEMA flood zones and Florida freeboard requirements — not a site-specific elevation lookup.
This does not tell you your actual required elevation. Base Flood Elevation (BFE) is address-specific and comes from the FEMA FIRM panel for that property. Local freeboard requirements are set at the municipal level and vary by city, not just by county. Always confirm both directly with the local building or floodplain management department.
