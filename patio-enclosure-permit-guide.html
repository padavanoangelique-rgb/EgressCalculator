Patio Enclosure Permit Guide

Screen rooms, sunrooms, and patio covers — how the type and the property type change what's required.
Reference only, not a permitting determination. Requirements vary by municipality and, for condos, by the individual association's governing documents. Confirm specifics with the local building department and, for condos, the association before starting design.

Property Type

Applies Regardless of Type or Property

Wind & Structural: Any attached or freestanding structure needs an engineered wind-load design (footings, anchoring, uplift) sized to the site's wind speed — this isn't optional even for "just a screen room."
HVHZ / Impact Glazing: In Miami-Dade, Broward, or a Wind-Borne Debris Region elsewhere, any new glazed opening needs product approval (NOA/FPA) rated for the site.
Zoning & Setbacks: Enclosures still have to clear side/rear setback requirements and any easements — a survey showing the proposed location is often required with the application.
NOC: Most enclosure jobs exceed Florida's Notice of Commencement threshold — check the NOC Threshold Checker for the specific job value.
